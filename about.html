About VIDZA Player

Engineered by SkeltoLabs • Unified Android & TV Media Core • Non-Intrusive Ads & Local Privacy

1. Our Mission: Playback Without Compromise

VIDZA Player was created with a clear objective: to restore purity, performance, and control to media playback on Android. In an era where media apps are bloated with invasive telemetry, forced cloud logins, and battery-draining background services, VIDZA Player takes the opposite approach.

We build software for media purists, cinephiles, and everyday viewers who value instant startup times, precise audio tuning, full subtitle customization, and total respect for device resources.

2. The Single Unified Engine Philosophy

Many media applications maintain separate, fragmented codebases for mobile phones and televisions, resulting in inconsistent bug fixes, uneven performance, and degraded TV interfaces.

VIDZA Player is architected around a single unified media engine powered by Google Media3 and native FFmpeg integration. The exact same rock-solid decoder pipeline, audio processing DSP, and playlist logic powers:

  • Android Smartphones: Intuitive touch gestures, swipe brightness, swipe volume, and double-tap seeking.
  • Android TV & Google TV: Native television interface with comprehensive D-pad remote navigation and landscape-first presentation.

3. Studio-Grade Audio & Video Engineering

Great video playback is incomplete without exceptional audio. VIDZA incorporates a custom 32-bit floating point PCM audio processor featuring:

  • 5 Precision Calibrated EQ Bands: 60Hz (Sub-Bass), 230Hz (Warmth), 910Hz (Midrange / Vocals), 3.6kHz (Dialogue Clarity), and 14kHz (Air & Treble).
  • Dialogue Boost & Volume Leveling: Up to +200% volume amplification with an integrated dynamic limiter to prevent clipping and protect sensitive speakers.
  • Hardware Decoder Acceleration: Seamless hardware decoding for H.264, HEVC (H.265), VP9, and AV1 with automatic fallback to FFmpeg software decoding for rare legacy containers.

4. Complete Local Privacy

VIDZA Player is an offline-first tool. What you watch, listen to, and store on your hardware belongs exclusively to you. We operate zero cloud collection servers, track zero watch history, and bundle zero third-party analytics trackers. Non-intrusive ads are managed transparently via Google AdMob without inspecting your personal media files.

5. About SkeltoLabs

SkeltoLabs is an independent software engineering studio focused on developing high-performance, privacy-respecting client applications for Android and Google TV. We build tools that run fast, use minimal memory, and do exactly what they promise without unnecessary distractions.
