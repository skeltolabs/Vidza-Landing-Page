1. General Product Nature & Scope

VIDZA Player is an offline-capable, general-purpose media rendering software application developed by SkeltoLabs.

Software Utility Notice

VIDZA Player is purely an audiovisual player engine. It functions in the same legal and technical category as a web browser or operating system media renderer. SkeltoLabs does not host, curate, publish, stream, transmit, or distribute any media content whatsoever.

2. Zero Content Hosting & DMCA Compliance

VIDZA Player contains zero built-in channels, zero online playlists, zero video catalogues, and zero access keys to copyrighted media. Any video or audio file rendered inside VIDZA Player is supplied directly by the end-user from:

  • The user's local device storage (internal memory, SD card, USB OTG drive);
  • The user's local network storage (SMB/LAN home NAS server); or
  • A direct, user-specified remote network stream URL (RTSP, HLS, DASH).

Because SkeltoLabs has no control over, stores no copies of, and cannot inspect files or streams opened locally by users, SkeltoLabs cannot remove or take down third-party files. Copyright holders seeking removal of infringing content must contact the host or distributor hosting the underlying file.

3. Hardware & Codec Performance Disclaimers

While VIDZA Player utilizes both Android MediaCodec hardware decoders and software FFmpeg decoders, playback performance (such as 4K UHD 60fps, 10-bit HEVC, AV1, or Dolby Vision) is inherently dependent upon the capabilities of your device's physical CPU and GPU.

SkeltoLabs does not guarantee that every media container, audio bitrate, or exotic video codec will achieve smooth playback on all hardware configurations or budget Android TV chipsets.

4. Audio Amplification & Output Safety

The built-in Loudness Booster (+200%) and 5-Band Equalizer are tools designed to enhance quiet dialogues and calibrate sound output. The user assumes full responsibility for maintaining volume levels within safe operating limits for both human hearing health and physical speaker transducer longevity.
